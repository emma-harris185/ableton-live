## 🎵 Music Production & Live Performance

**Ableton Live** is a digital audio workstation designed for music creation, recording, arrangement, sound design, mixing and live performance.

Its workflow combines traditional timeline-based production with a flexible Session View.

---

# 🎼 SESSION VIEW

Experiment with musical ideas without committing immediately to a linear arrangement.

Work with:

* Clips
* Scenes
* Loops
* Samples
* MIDI
* Instruments
* Effects

```text id="alsession"
CLIP → CLIP → CLIP
  ↓      ↓      ↓
SCENE → SCENE → SCENE
  ↓
LIVE PERFORMANCE
```
